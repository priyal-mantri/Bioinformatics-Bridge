  Observations:
    - NHANES is a US-based survey; ethnicity distribution reflects
      the US adult population, not Indian subcontinent demographics.
    - Sex differences are expected in body composition (BMD, lean mass,
      body fat %) and lipid profiles (HDL typically higher in females).
    - Age-related trends are expected in blood pressure, BMD, and
      metabolic markers -- these are biological, not confounders per se.
    - The clustering is unsupervised; demographic variables are NOT
      included in the feature matrix. Post-hoc analysis should check
      whether clusters are disproportionately age- or sex-stratified.

  Recommendation:
    No demographic adjustment is recommended before clustering.
    Post-clustering: compute demographic composition of each cluster
    to assess whether clusters are confounded by age or sex.
